How We Prioritise the Backlog

A simpler scoring model. Good enough to make decisions.

The Problem

We score to decide, not to be right

The question isn't "is the estimate accurate?" It's "is it good enough to choose what comes next?"

Two Inputs

Two questions.
One score.

Stakeholder
What is this worth?
The Product Owner works with stakeholders to agree a value score. The conversation happens before scoring, not during it.
Team
How hard is this?
The team scores effort relative to other work. Not time. A feature and a story can sit side by side and be compared directly.
Applies to
Features and stories
The same scale works at both levels. Scores are relative, so a feature-level estimate and a story-level estimate use the same numbers.
Scale
1, 2, 3, 5
No 4, no 8, no Fibonacci drama. Small enough to keep discussions short.
The Formula

Value ÷ Effort × RS = Score

Value and Effort are scored 1–5. RS is a regulatory multiplier tied to the deadline field in the ticket. No RS? Default is 1.

Stakeholder Scoring

Value
Scale

A straight 1–4 scale. Different from the effort scale deliberately, so the two don't get confused. It's a judgement call, not a calculation.

1 . Low
Barely anyone affected. Consequence of deferring is minimal.
2 . Medium
Some users affected, or a workaround exists but creates friction.
3 . High
Meaningful number of users affected, or a key stakeholder is blocked.
4 . Critical
Broad impact, no workaround, or a significant relationship is at risk.
Team Scoring

Effort
Scale

Relative, not time-based. Score it against other items in the backlog, not against a calendar.

1 . Simple
Clear work, known skills, no surprises expected.
2 . Lots of work
Volume is high but the team knows what it involves.
3 . Dependencies
Something outside the team is involved. That makes it riskier.
5 . Big and unclear
Complex, unknown scope, possible learning curve.
0 . Too many unknowns
Needs a spike or discovery conversation before it can be scored.
Regulatory Scoring

Deadlines move the score

Default
RS = 1
Not regulatory. Score is driven entirely by value and effort.
Deadline > 6 months
RS = 3 × Effort
On the radar. Regulatory weight starts to influence priority.
Deadline within 6 months
RS = 5 × Effort
Getting close. Regulatory work moves up the order.
Imminent or missed
RS = 10
This goes to the top. The deadline field in the ticket drives this automatically.
What's Next

Flow metrics come
later

We don't have throughput or cycle time data yet. Once we have some flow history, we can use it to understand delivery cadence. For now, the score is enough to sequence the work.

In Summary

Simple enough
to use.

Value from stakeholders. Effort from the team. Deadlines handled by the ticket. One formula. One ranked list.
